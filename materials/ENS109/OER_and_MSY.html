📘 Learning corner
Maximum Sustainable Yield (MSY) = largest catch that can be taken indefinitely without reducing the population.
For logistic growth: MSY = r·K/4 achieved when population = K/2 and harvest rate E = r/2.
Optimal extraction rate balances growth: if E > r, population crashes. Below r/2 → under-harvesting.
