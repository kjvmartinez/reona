"My smoke harms your health... but I don't pay for it!"

📉 Externalities externality

Unpriced side effects — when a factory pollutes a river, that's a negative externality. Society bears the cost.
🐟🐟🚤
"If I don't take more fish, someone else will!"

⚖️ Tragedy of the Commons commons

Shared resources (fisheries, forests) get overexploited because individual gain > shared loss. 💡 Pigouvian tax / tradeable permits help.
💨💵🌎
"Pollute less or pay the price!"

🏷️ Carbon Pricing cap & trade

Market-based approach to reduce emissions. Cap & trade creates scarcity for pollution permits → companies reduce efficiently.
🐝🌸💧
"We clean water & air — for free!"

🌱 Ecosystem Services natural capital

Benefits from nature: pollination, water filtration, carbon storage. Assigning economic value helps protect them.
⏳👵🌲
"Should we care less about future generations?"

⏱️ Discount Rate intergenerational equity

How we value future costs/benefits today. Crucial for climate policy — a low discount rate favors strong action now.
🔄🗑️♻️
"Waste = Food! Let's close the loop."

🔄 Circular Economy zero waste

Designing out waste, keeping materials in use. Regenerative system — alternative to linear economy.
