📚 What is MCDA?

Multiple Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) is a structured approach used to evaluate and rank options when decisions involve multiple, often conflicting, criteria. It helps decision-makers quantify preferences and trade-offs.

🔑 Core Steps:

  1. Define alternatives – the choices you are comparing.
  2. Identify criteria – attributes that matter (cost, quality, sustainability, etc.).
  3. Assign weights – indicate relative importance (sum to 100% or 1).
  4. Score alternatives – rate how each alternative performs on each criterion (e.g., 1-10).
  5. Calculate weighted scores – sum(criterion_score × weight).
  6. Rank & decide – highest total score is the preferred option.
📐 Weighted Sum Model (WSM) formula:
\( S_i = \sum_{j=1}^{n} w_j \times x_{ij} \)
Where \(w_j\) = weight of criterion j, \(x_{ij}\) = score of alternative i on criterion j.

💡 Tip: Normalization is sometimes used when criteria have different units. In this module we use direct rating (1–10) for simplicity, but you can adapt.
