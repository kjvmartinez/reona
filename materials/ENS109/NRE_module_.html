📖 Core concepts

Natural resource economics studies how scarce environmental resources are allocated over time. Key trade-off: extract today vs. preserve for future.

🏭 Hotelling’s Rule (1931)
For a non‑renewable resource, the price (or net price) must rise at the rate of interest (discount rate) to ensure efficient intertemporal allocation.
Pₜ = P₀ · e^(r·t)   or   (Pₜ₊₁ - Pₜ)/Pₜ = r
Higher discount rate → faster extraction today.
🌲 Renewable vs Non‑renewable
• Renewable: Fish, forests — can regenerate if harvest ≤ growth rate (Maximum Sustainable Yield).
• Non‑renewable: Oil, minerals — fixed stock; depletion implies scarcity rent.
💰 Discounting & Future Value
Future benefits are discounted: PV = FutureValue / (1+r)^t.
High discount rates favor immediate extraction, low rates favor conservation.
