🌱 How to Play

🎯 Your Mission

You are the manager of an eco-resort. Your goal is to balance profit with environmental protection and community well-being.

📊 Game Stats

  • 🌿 Sustainability Score: Your total points. Make wise choices to increase it!
  • 📈 Eco-Meter: Shows how healthy your environment is.
    ⚠️ Fragile (0-33%) ⚖️ Balanced (34-66%) 🌟 Thriving (67-100%)

🎮 How to Play

  • 1. Read the Scenario: Each round presents a real-world ecotourism challenge.
  • 2. Choose Wisely: Tap on one of the three choices (A, B, or C).
  • 3. Learn from Feedback: See how your choice affected your score and eco-meter. Read the detailed explanation!
  • 4. Next Challenge: Tap "NEXT CHALLENGE" to face a new situation.
  • 5. Keep Playing: The game randomly selects from 10+ scenarios. Try to achieve the highest sustainability score!

💡 Pro Tips

✅ Think like an ecotourist: What benefits nature AND the local community?

✅ Balance is key: Sometimes the most profitable choice harms the environment.

✅ Learn from mistakes: If your eco-meter drops, you can recover with better choices!

✅ Real-world lessons: Every scenario is based on actual ecotourism challenges.

🏆 Goal

Become an EcoGuardian Champion by making decisions that create a sustainable, thriving resort that benefits nature, local communities, and your business!
